# AppVellum

AppVellum is a job application tracking web application designed to help students and job seekers organise, manage, and monitor their job and internship applications in one centralised platform.

## Problem Statement

As a student and job seeker, I need a centralised platform to track and manage my job and internship applications, monitor the progress of each application, keep track of important dates and deadlines, and organise relevant information in one place. Without a centralised system, it can be difficult to keep track of multiple applications and their current statuses, which may lead to missed deadlines, forgotten follow-ups, or disorganised application information.
